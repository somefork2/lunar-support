FAQ

Why does my lunar day differ from another calendar? Lunar days start at moonrise, which depends on your location. Set your city in Settings → Place for exact times.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Lunar Calendar → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid a charge.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings in the app → Restore purchases.

Is it scientific? Astronomy (phases, moonrise, signs, eclipses) is precise. Advice follows folk and astrological lunar-calendar traditions and is not a substitute for a doctor or other professional.
