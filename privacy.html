Privacy Policy

Lunar Calendar: Best Days (“the app”). Effective date: September 23, 2026.

Data we collect

None. Your city, favorite activities, crops and journal entries are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers and are not shared with anyone. All astronomical calculations run on the device.

Location

If you tap “Detect automatically”, the app uses your approximate location once to find your city and calculate moonrise times. The location is not stored beyond the city coordinates saved on your device and is never sent to us. City search text is sent to Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy.

Calendar and notifications

With your permission the app can add an event to your calendar (write-only access) and schedule local reminders. Nothing leaves your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details; the app only checks whether your access is active.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Deleting data

Long-press a journal entry to delete it, or remove the app to erase all data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
